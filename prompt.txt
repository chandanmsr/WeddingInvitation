23. MOTION DESIGN
==================================================

Use Framer Motion intentionally.

Allowed animations:
- Fade and small vertical movement
- Slow decorative floating
- Gentle stagger for event cards
- Scale from 0.98 to 1
- Section reveals
- Light line-drawing effects
- Invitation-cover opening
- Subtle parallax

Avoid:
- Constant large movement
- Rapid movement
- Excessive bouncing
- Confetti on every section
- Animating long paragraphs character by character
- Large background videos on mobile
- Scroll-jacking
- Custom cursors that reduce usability

Create reusable animation variants.

Use Intersection Observer or viewport-based motion so animations run only when relevant.

Respect:
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)

When reduced motion is enabled:
- Disable parallax
- Disable floating petals
- Remove looping decorative movement
- Use immediate or simple fade transitions
- Keep all content accessible
